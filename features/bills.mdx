title: "View and manage your billing history and records"
description: "Browse recent electricity bills and payment history, filter by date range, check payment status, export data to CSV, and quickly navigate to pay an outstanding bill."
---

The **Bills** page gives you a complete record of your electricity charges and payment activity in one place. It loads two sections simultaneously — **Recent Bills** and **Payment History** — so you can cross-reference charges and payments without switching pages.

## Recent bills

The Recent Bills table lists every bill generated for your account. Each row contains the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Period** | The billing period the charge covers (e.g., `Nov 2025`) |
| **Bill ID** | Unique identifier for the bill (e.g., `BILL-2025-11`) |
| **Amount** | Total amount charged for the period |
| **Status** | Whether the bill has been paid or is still outstanding |
| **Due Date** | The date by which payment must be received |

### Bill status badges

Each bill displays a status badge next to the amount:

- **Paid** (green) — payment has been received and processed
- **Unpaid** (yellow) — the bill is outstanding and may be due or overdue

### Pay a bill

Click **Pay a Bill** at the top of the page to go directly to the payment form. The payment page opens the secure payment gateway where you can settle an outstanding balance. See [Making a payment](/features/payments) for a full walkthrough.

## Payment history

The Payment History table shows every transaction made on your account, including the payment method used and the final transaction status.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Date** | The date the transaction was processed |
| **Transaction ID** | Unique identifier for the transaction (e.g., `TXN-839182`) |
| **Method** | The payment method used (e.g., credit card) |
| **Amount** | The amount that was charged |
| **Status** | The outcome of the transaction |

## Filtering by date range

Both tables share a date range filter at the top of the page. Use the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields to narrow results:

- **Recent Bills** — filters by the bill's period date
- **Payment History** — filters by the transaction date

Enter a start date, an end date, or both. The table updates immediately to show only the records that fall within the selected range. Clear either field to remove that boundary and show all records on that side of the range.

## Exporting data

Each table has a **Download CSV** button. Click it to download a CSV file containing all currently visible rows — including any active date filter. Use exported files for personal recordkeeping, tax purposes, or to share with your accountant.

<Tip>
  Apply a date filter before clicking **Download CSV** to export only the records you need, such as all bills from the previous calendar year.
</Tip>
