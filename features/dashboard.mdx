The dashboard is your home base in Smart CX. It loads your account information automatically on first visit and gives you an at-a-glance summary of your electricity account.

## What you see on the dashboard

The dashboard is organized into four main areas: a balance summary at the top, a **Pay Bill** quick action, an **Account Details** card, and a **Service Address** card.

### Balance summary

Your current balance is displayed prominently at the top of the page with the currency symbol (e.g., `$42.75`). The due date appears directly below it so you always know when your next payment is expected.

### Pay Bill

The **Pay Bill** button appears next to the balance summary. Clicking it takes you directly to the payment page where you can complete a payment using a saved method.

<Tip>
  If your balance shows `$0.00`, there is nothing due. The **Pay Bill** button is still accessible if you want to make an advance payment.
</Tip>

## Account details card

The **Account Details** card shows the identifying information for your electricity account.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Account ID** | Your unique account identifier (e.g., `ELEC-100234`) |
| **Plan** | Your current rate plan (e.g., `Standard Residential`) |
| **Status** | Whether your account is `active`, `suspended`, or another status |
| **Meter number** | The meter ID associated with your account |

## Service address card

The **Service Address** card shows the physical address where electricity service is delivered.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Label** | A friendly name for the address (e.g., `Home`, `Office`) |
| **Street** | Street number and name |
| **City** | City |
| **State** | State abbreviation |
| **ZIP** | ZIP code |

<Tip>
  If your account has multiple service addresses, a dropdown selector appears at the top of the **Service Address** card. Use it to switch between addresses and view each location's details.
</Tip>

## How account data loads

When you navigate to the dashboard, Smart CX automatically fetches your account data from `GET /utility/electricity/account`. No manual refresh is needed — the page populates as soon as your session is verified.

If your profile is linked to multiple electricity accounts, an **Account** selector appears in the header bar at the top of every page. Use it to switch between accounts from anywhere in the portal — the dashboard and all other pages update to reflect the selected account.

<Note>
  Account data is fetched once on first load. If you need to see the latest information after making changes, refresh the page.
</Note>
