---
title: "Make a payment securely through the payment gateway"
description: "Pay an outstanding electricity bill through the secure iframe payment gateway, choose your payment method, and track the result — all without leaving the portal."
---

The **Payment** page lets you settle an outstanding bill through a secure, isolated payment gateway. The gateway runs inside an iframe so your payment details are handled entirely by the payment service and never pass through the portal itself.

## Payment flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the payment page">
    From the **Bills** page, click **Pay a Bill**. You can also navigate directly to `/payment` from the main menu.

    The portal checks whether the payment service is reachable before loading the form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your payment details">
    If the payment service is online, the payment form loads inside a secure iframe. The portal passes the following context to the form automatically — you do not need to enter these manually:

    - Your **account ID**
    - Your **current balance**
    - Your **currency**
    - Your **service address**
    - The **payment methods** available on your account

    Select a payment method, enter the required details, and confirm the payment.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the result">
    After you submit the form, one of two things happens:

    - **Payment successful** — the portal records the transaction against your account and your balance updates immediately. You are returned to the previous page.
    - **Payment cancelled** — if you click cancel inside the payment form, the portal discards the session and returns you to the previous page with no changes made.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported payment methods

The portal retrieves available payment methods from `GET /utility/payment/methods`. The methods available to you depend on your account configuration. The following method is supported:

| Method | Key |
|---|---|
| Credit card | `credit_card` |

<Note>
  Your account may be configured with additional payment methods. The list displayed in the payment form reflects exactly what your utility provider has enabled for your account.
</Note>

## When the payment service is unavailable

The portal checks connectivity to the payment service before rendering the iframe. If the service cannot be reached, you will see a **Payment System Unavailable** message instead of the payment form.

From that screen you have two options:

- **Retry** — attempts to reconnect to the payment service and reload the form
- **Return** — takes you back to the previous page without making a payment

<Warning>
  If the payment service is unavailable, no payment can be processed. Try again in a few minutes or contact your utility provider if the issue persists.
</Warning>

## Error scenarios

Errors can occur after the payment form is submitted. The following outcomes are possible:

| Error | Cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Timeout | The payment service did not respond in time | Retry the payment; check your transaction history before retrying to avoid a duplicate charge |
| Payment declined (402) | The payment was declined by your card issuer | Verify your card details or try a different payment method |
| Server error (500) | An unexpected error occurred on the payment service | Wait a moment and retry; contact support if the error continues |

<Tip>
  Before retrying after a timeout, check your **Payment History** on the [Bills page](/features/bills) to confirm whether the original payment was recorded. This avoids paying twice for the same bill.
</Tip>
