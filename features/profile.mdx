The profile page shows your customer information and lets you manage the service addresses associated with your account.

## Personal information

The top section of the profile page displays your contact details pulled from your account record.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **First name** | Customer first name |
| **Last name** | Customer last name |
| **Email** | Email address on file |
| **Phone** | Phone number on file |

<Note>
  The **Edit Profile** button is visible on this page but is not yet implemented. Profile editing (name, email, phone) is planned for a future release.
</Note>

## Service addresses

Your service addresses are listed below your personal information. If you have more than one address, a dropdown selector lets you switch between them to view each address's details.

Each address entry shows:

- **Label** — a friendly name (e.g., `Home`, `Cabin`)
- **Street** — street number and name
- **City**, **State**, **ZIP** — location details

## Adding a new service address

Use the **Add New** button to open an inline form and register an additional service address on your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Add New** on the profile page. An inline form appears directly below the existing addresses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the address label">
    Type a short, recognizable name for this address in the **Label** field (e.g., `Vacation Home`). The label helps you identify the address in dropdowns across the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the street address">
    Fill in the **Street** field with the full street number and name (e.g., `742 Evergreen Terrace`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter city, state, and ZIP">
    Complete the **City**, **State**, and **ZIP** fields for the address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click the submit button in the form. The new address is added to your account immediately and appears in the service address list and dropdown selector.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  After adding an address, you can switch to it using the dropdown on both the profile page and the dashboard's **Service Address** card.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Address changes made in the portal update your local session. Contact your utility provider's support team if you need to make permanent address changes that persist across sessions.
</Note>
