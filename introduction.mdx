Smart CX is a web-based customer experience portal designed for utility companies and their customers. It provides a unified interface where residential and commercial customers can view their electricity account details, pay bills, monitor outage status, submit meter readings, and get help through an AI-powered support assistant.

## How it works

Smart CX is a React single-page application that communicates with a backend mock API. Authentication uses JWT access and refresh tokens — your session is automatically maintained so you stay logged in without interruption.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Navigate to your Smart CX portal URL. You'll be directed to the secure login screen. Enter your credentials to receive an access token and refresh token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your dashboard">
    After login, the dashboard shows your current balance, due date, plan, meter number, and service address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage your account">
    Use the navigation menu to access bills, payments, services, outages, and support from any page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you need to activate service at a new property, you can start the process without logging in by visiting the **Start Service** page directly.
</Note>
