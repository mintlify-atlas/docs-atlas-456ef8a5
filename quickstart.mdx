Smart CX is your all-in-one utility account portal. Whether you need to check your current balance, pay an outstanding bill, or report a power outage, every action is a few clicks away. This guide walks you through logging in for the first time, orienting yourself on the dashboard, and completing a core task so you can get on with your day.

<Note>
  You can start service at a new address without logging in. Visit the **Start Service** page from the portal's home screen and follow the guided wizard — no account required.
</Note>

## Before you begin

You need two things before you log in:

- **A Smart CX account.** Your utility provider creates this when you sign up for service. Contact your provider if you have not received your account credentials.
- **Your portal URL.** This is the web address your utility provider gave you (for example, `https://portal.yourprovider.com`). Bookmark it for easy access.

## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in for the first time">
    Open your portal URL in a browser. You will be redirected to the secure login screen.

    Enter the **email address** and **password** associated with your Smart CX account, then click **Sign in**.

    Smart CX authenticates you using a secure JWT session. Once verified, you are taken directly to your dashboard.

    <Note>
      Your session persists across page refreshes. After 5 minutes of inactivity, a session timeout warning appears with a 60-second countdown. Click **Keep Session Alive** to continue, or **Sign Out** to end the session immediately.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate the dashboard">
    The dashboard gives you an at-a-glance summary of your account:

    - **Current balance** — the amount owed on your latest bill
    - **Due date** — when payment is expected
    - **Plan** — your active service plan
    - **Meter number** — the identifier for your meter
    - **Service address** — the property associated with your account

    Use the navigation menu to move between sections: **Bills**, **Services**, **Outages**, **Payment**, and **Profile**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay your first bill">
    1. Select **Bills** from the navigation menu to view your billing history.
    2. Locate an outstanding bill and click **Pay now**.
    3. You will be taken to the secure **Payment** page, which loads a payment form in an isolated iframe.
    4. Enter your payment details and confirm. Your payment is recorded and your balance updates immediately.

    <Tip>
      Once a payment is processed, you can return to **Bills** to download a record of the transaction.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit a meter reading">
    If your account requires manual meter submissions, navigate to **Services** from the main menu.

    1. Click **Submit meter reading**.
    2. Enter the current reading from your physical meter.
    3. Click **Submit**. The reading is sent to your utility provider and logged under your service history.

    <Tip>
      You can also open a service request from this page if you need to report an issue with your meter or connection.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>
